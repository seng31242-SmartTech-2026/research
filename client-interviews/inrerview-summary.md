# Interview Summary

## Chapter 3: System Analysis

### 3.1.1 Structured Interviews

Five structured interviews were conducted with potential Buyers and Sellers of technology products in Sri Lanka between January and February 2026. Each interview lasted approximately **30–45 minutes**.

## Interview Guide (Key Questions)

1. What online platforms do you currently use to buy or sell mobile phones and laptops?  
   *(e.g., Daraz, ikman.lk, Facebook Marketplace)*

2. What are the biggest frustrations you experience when buying tech products online?

3. How do you currently verify the authenticity and condition of a second-hand device before purchasing?

4. What payment methods do you prefer, and what concerns do you have about online payments?

5. As a seller, what tools would make managing your listings and orders easier?

6. What features would make you trust and prefer SmartTech over existing platforms?

## Key Findings from Interviews

- **80%** of interviewees expressed frustration with irrelevant search results on general marketplaces when searching specifically for technology products.

- **60%** of potential sellers cited the lack of a proper storefront and analytics as a major barrier to selling online.

- **100%** of interviewees expressed concerns regarding product authenticity. A **verified seller badge** and **review system** were rated as highly important features.

- **Card payment** and **bank transfer** were identified as the most preferred payment methods.

- **Order tracking visibility** was highlighted as a top priority by buyers.
